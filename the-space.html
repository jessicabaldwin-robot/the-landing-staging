The Space

Where Missoula teams collaborate

Approximately 1,235 total square feet at 269 West Front Street, set up to reconfigure between a board meeting, a classroom, and a reception.

845 square feet

Common Area

The common area is the main room at The Landing and the reason most groups book it. White oak slat walls run along one side, a TV is on hand for presentations, and three arched windows sit at the far end above the cafe seating. The floor is wide-plank and clear, so tables roll where you need them.

The bar runs along the back: counter, sink, espresso machine, pendant lighting, and a kegerator available for events. Groups use it as a coffee station through a morning meeting and as a real bar for an evening reception, without moving to a different room.

  • 845 square feet of configurable floor space
  • Rolling tables and movable seating for boardroom, classroom, or reception layouts
  • TV with soundbar for presentations
  • Coffee and cocktail bar with counter and sink
  • Espresso machine
  • Kegerator available for events
  • Cafe seating along the window wall
  • Bring your own catering, no exclusive vendor requirement
195 square feet each

Two Conference Rooms

The two conference rooms open off the common area through oak French doors with transom windows above, so daylight carries through and the floor still reads as one connected space. Leave the doors open and a large group flows between all three rooms. Close them and a committee gets a quiet room without leaving the building.

Each room holds modular tables with rolling chairs and a TV for video calls. The tables reconfigure the same way the common area does, so a room can run as a small boardroom, a classroom, or a theater setup depending on the session. They work for breakout groups, interview panels, a hybrid call running alongside a larger meeting, or a quiet place for a speaker to prep.

  • Approximately 195 square feet with its own tables and rolling chairs
  • Modular tables that reconfigure for boardroom, classroom, or theater setups
  • TV for presenting and video conferencing
  • Wi-Fi throughout
  • Oak French doors that open to the common area or close for privacy
  • Works as a breakout room alongside a larger meeting
Configurations

Modular Layout Options

Tell us which one you want in your inquiry and the room will be set that way when you arrive.

Boardroom

One long table down the center of the common area with the monitor in view from every seat. Best for board meetings, committee work, and partner meetings.

Classroom

Rolling tables in rows facing the screen, with the bar open for coffee at the back. Best for trainings, CE sessions, workshops, and onboarding.

Theater

Chairs in rows facing the screen with the tables cleared away. Best for presentations, panels, and annual meetings when the room needs to seat as many people as possible.

Reception

Tables cleared, cocktail rounds set, kegerator tapped, cafe seating in use. Best for client events, member mixers, donor receptions, and after-hours gatherings.

Getting here

Getting to The Landing

The Landing is at 269 West Front Street in downtown Missoula, a block from the Clark Fork and next to Caras Park. The Caras Park lot is the most convenient parking for guests, and Front Street is an easy walk from most of downtown, which matters when half your attendees are already working within a few blocks.

If your group is coming from out of town, The Landing is a short drive from Missoula Montana Airport and walking distance from downtown hotels and restaurants, so you can put a training here and let people find lunch on their own.

Before you book

Frequently Asked Questions

The details Missoula organizers ask about most, from parking and capacity to catering and AV.

Where is The Landing and where do guests park?

The Landing is at 269 West Front Street in downtown Missoula. The Caras Park lot is the most convenient parking for guests, and the entrance is a short walk from there.

How many people can The Landing hold?

The common area is 845 square feet and the two conference rooms are 195 square feet each, which covers everything from a board table to a standing reception. Final capacity depends on your layout, so tell us your headcount in the inquiry and we will confirm the setup that fits.

What does it cost to rent The Landing?

Rates depend on the day, the length of the booking, and the setup you need, so we quote each event rather than publish a rate card. Send an inquiry and we will get pricing back to you.

Can we bring our own food and drinks?

Yes. The Landing has no exclusive catering contract, so bring the caterer you like or pick up lunch down the street. The bar includes an espresso machine, and a kegerator is available for events.

Is there technology for presentations and video calls?

Yes. Wi-Fi covers the whole space, and each conference room has a TV monitor for presenting and video conferencing, so a hybrid meeting works without anyone hauling in equipment.

Can the room be set up differently than the photos show?

Yes. The tables and seating in the common area are movable, so the same room runs as a boardroom, a classroom, or an open reception floor. Note your preferred layout in the inquiry.

Do you offer anything for nonprofits?

Yes. For nonprofit groups with simple setup needs, The Landing is often available at no cost. Tell us about your organization in the inquiry and we will let you know what we can do for your date.

Do you host personal celebrations like showers or birthday parties?

The Landing is set up for professional gatherings: meetings, trainings, workshops, and business or nonprofit events. If you are planning a personal celebration, we are happy to point you toward other Missoula venues that are a better fit.
